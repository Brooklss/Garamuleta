— OUR STORY —

Twenty Years of Hot Water Expertise

Founded in 2004 by Ato Tekeba Brhine — growing from a small Addis Ababa import operation to Ethiopia's most trusted name in bathroom water heating solutions.

Tekcon Ethiopia was founded in 2004 by Ato Tekeba Brhine with a single mission: bring reliable, affordable hot water to every Ethiopian home. Over two decades, we have grown from a one-person import business into the country's leading bathroom water heating solutions company.

We specialize in importing, distributing, installing, and servicing all types of water heaters — electric storage (10L to 80L), instant electric, solar evacuated-tube systems, and gas heaters. Every product is carefully selected for Ethiopian conditions: reliable power performance, corrosion resistance, and energy efficiency.

From individual apartments in Addis Ababa to large hotels in Hawassa and Bahir Dar, Tekcon has delivered hot water to thousands of Ethiopian families — backed by professional installation, genuine spare parts, and after-sales service that clients trust.
