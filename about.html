— OUR STORY —

A Construction Legacy That Built Ethiopia's Hottest Brand

Tekcon started as a construction company 20 years ago — and that foundation of engineering excellence is exactly what made us the biggest and best bathroom water heating solution in Ethiopia today.

Tekcon was born in construction. Twenty years ago, we were building Ethiopia's future — erecting buildings, infrastructure, and homes across the country. In doing so, we saw firsthand a challenge that every Ethiopian faces: the lack of reliable, high-quality hot water. We knew we could solve it better than anyone else.

So we pivoted with purpose. Leveraging our deep engineering roots, industry relationships, and two decades of operational expertise, Tekcon became laser-focused on bathroom water heating. We partnered with Midea — the world's largest and most awarded water heater manufacturer — to bring global-standard technology to every Ethiopian home.

Today, Tekcon is the biggest and best bathroom water heating solution in Ethiopia. With over 10,000 units installed across homes, hotels, hospitals, and commercial buildings from Addis Ababa to Bahir Dar, we have earned our position at the top — and we intend to keep it.
